# Babarify Media Player 0.10.0 (public beta)

The player's new name is Babarify Media Player; it was called Babarify Frame. This beta adds a music library,
a new look and many features asked for since 0.9.1. It is free, makes no network connection of its own, and
runs on Windows 10 (1809) and 11, 64-bit. Licensed under the GNU GPL v3 or later.

## Install or update

Run `BabarifyMediaPlayer-0.10.0-setup.exe`. It installs for your Windows user only, with no administrator
rights, into `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Babarify Media Player`. An earlier Babarify Frame is moved there, and
its old folder is removed; your settings, library, history and playlists are kept. Close the player first.
A taskbar pin to the old copy has to be pinned again.

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it doesn't recognise the app. Choose
*More info → Run anyway* only if the file's SHA-256 matches the published `SHA256SUMS.txt`:

    Get-FileHash .\BabarifyMediaPlayer-0.10.0-setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256

The first time it runs, the library reads your songs once more in the background, for their tags, pictures
and year. To go back to 0.9.1, run its installer: it replaces the program, and 0.9.1 starts with an empty
library because it can't read the newer one. Your data stays on disk for when you update again.

## Music

- **Library tabs** for Songs, Albums, Artists, Playlists and **For you**, from your folders' tags and pictures,
  with search, sorting (including newest, oldest, most played, year and folder), Play and Shuffle.
- **An audio screen** with the song's picture and colours taken from it, or an animation when it has none.
- **Queue:** Play next and Add to queue from a song's menu; shuffle and repeat (off, this file or all).
- **Favourites** and automatic Recently played, Most played and Recently added lists.
- **For you:** a recap of what you've played and for how long, your top songs, artists and albums, and songs
  you've never played. Worked out on your PC.
- **Lyrics** from an `.lrc` or text file, or typed in. With times, they follow the song (Playback → Lyrics, or Y).
- **Edit details:** correct a song's title, artist, album or cover. Kept by the app; your files aren't changed.
- **Fade between songs** in a list, and a sound effect: room, wider stereo or both (Settings → Playback).
- **Sleep timer**, and an equalizer with Bass boost and Preamp dials.

## Library and files

- **Hide** files from the library, and bring them back in Settings → Privacy → Hidden files.
- **Delete from disk**, which asks first and moves the file to the Recycle Bin.
- **Playlists:** import and export M3U files.
- **Backup:** save your settings, playlists, favourites and hidden files to a file, and restore them on this or
  another PC (Settings → Privacy). What you played isn't in the file.

## Look

- **Seven colour themes**, including Light, with Theme and Animation buttons on the left bar for a quick change.
- **Your own picture** as the background of the main pages, dimmed so text stays readable.
- **Clearer controls:** larger player buttons with bolder icons, raised tabs and buttons, and a soft lift when
  you point at a button.
- Arial throughout, bold for titles and headings. The status bar is hidden unless you switch it on.

## Privacy

Unchanged: no account, no telemetry, no update check. Files are never changed; one is deleted only when you
choose *Delete from disk* and confirm. Edited details, covers, lyrics and a background picture are kept by the
app. See `PRIVACY.txt` in the program folder.

Known issues: `docs/beta/KNOWN_ISSUES.md`. Problem reports: Help → Report a problem.
